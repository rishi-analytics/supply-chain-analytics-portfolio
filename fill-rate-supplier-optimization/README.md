## Overview
Applied predictive and prescriptive analytics to evaluate supplier delivery performance and forecast future stockout risk within Prosacco’s supply chain network.

## Objective
- Calculate supplier fill rate performance
- Forecast undelivered quantities
- Identify alternative suppliers capable of achieving full delivery coverage

## Key Findings
- Problematic supplier fill rate: 94.7%
- Supplier 2 identified as a viable replacement supplier
- Supplier delivery performance directly impacted stock availability and service reliability

## Methodology
- Calculated fill rate using:
  - On-time delivered quantity / quantity ordered
- Forecasted future undelivered units using historical fill rate performance
- Evaluated supplier-city coverage overlap to identify replacement options

## Key Insight
The analysis demonstrated how supplier reliability and fill rate performance can be used to proactively predict stockout risk and optimize supplier allocation decisions.

- Excel / Google Sheets

- Prescriptive Analytics

## Outcome
Developed a data-driven supplier replacement recommendation to improve delivery reliability and reduce future stockout risk.
