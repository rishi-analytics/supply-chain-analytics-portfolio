# Inventory Strategy Optimization Using EOQ, JIT, Reorder Point, and ABC Analysis

## Business Context
As an analyst at Prosacco, the objective was to evaluate inventory management strategies that could reduce rising inventory costs while maintaining service reliability for critical raw materials and products.

The analysis focused on determining whether EOQ, JIT, reorder point planning, ABC analysis, and safety stock strategies could improve inventory efficiency and support supply chain decision-making.

---

## My Role
- Evaluated inventory management models using operational and demand data
- Calculated inventory control metrics including EOQ, safety stock, and reorder point
- Assessed trade-offs between inventory cost, stock availability, and supplier responsiveness
- Recommended inventory strategies aligned with operational efficiency goals

## Key Findings
- EOQ can reduce total inventory costs by balancing ordering frequency and holding costs
- JIT can reduce excess inventory but increases dependency on supplier reliability and forecasting accuracy
- Reorder point planning supports timely replenishment and lowers stockout risk
- ABC analysis enables prioritization of high-value inventory items for tighter control
- Safety stock helps absorb demand variability and maintain target service levels

---

## Key Insight
The analysis demonstrated that combining EOQ, reorder point planning, selective safety stock, and ABC prioritization can improve inventory efficiency while balancing service level targets and operational cost constraints.

---

## Recommendations
- Implement EOQ-based replenishment for stable-demand inventory
- Use reorder point monitoring for timely procurement decisions
- Apply JIT selectively where supplier reliability is consistent
- Prioritize monitoring of high-value A-category inventory
- Maintain safety stock buffers for demand variability protection

---

## Trade-Off Analysis

| Strategy | Benefit | Trade-Off |
|---|---|---|
| EOQ | Reduces total inventory cost | May increase inventory holding volume |
| JIT | Minimizes holding cost | Sensitive to supplier delays |
| Safety Stock | Reduces stockout risk | Increases carrying cost |
| ABC Analysis | Improves inventory focus | Requires differentiated inventory management |
