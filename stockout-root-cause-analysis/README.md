## Overview
Analyzed inventory and supplier delivery data to identify the root cause of recurring stockout issues affecting a high-demand SKU at Prosacco.

The project focused on comparing actual inventory levels against ideal stock targets and investigating supplier lead time performance to diagnose operational bottlenecks.

## Objective
- Identify the SKU experiencing the most significant stockout issues
- Compare actual vs ideal stock levels across SKUs
- Investigate supplier lead time performance
- Determine the likely root cause of stockouts

## Methodology
- Created pivot tables to analyze SKU-level stock performance
- Built data visualizations comparing actual stock and ideal stock
- Identified frequency and severity of stockout patterns
- Analyzed delivery report data to evaluate supplier lead time issues
- Connected supplier delays with inventory shortages

## Key Findings

### SKU Experiencing Major Stockouts
- OB1265

### Root Cause Identified
- Supplier 1 experienced delivery and lead time issues that likely contributed to recurring stockouts of SKU OB1265.

## Key Insight
The analysis revealed that stockout issues were not solely driven by inventory levels, but were strongly connected to supplier delivery performance and lead time variability.

## Recommendations
- Improve supplier performance monitoring
- Establish lead time tracking metrics
- Develop contingency sourcing strategies
- Maintain safety stock for high-risk SKUs

## Tools
- Excel / Google Sheets
- Pivot Tables
- Data Visualization
- Descriptive Analytics
- Diagnostic Analytics

## Outcome
Identified the operational root cause of recurring stockouts by combining inventory analysis with supplier performance evaluation, enabling more informed supply chain decision-making.
