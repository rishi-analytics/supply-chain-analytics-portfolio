# Supply Chain Support and Stockout Mitigation Analysis
## Business Context
As a supply chain analyst at Prosacco, the objective was to investigate stockout issues affecting a delayed SKU within the company’s lean supply chain model and evaluate operational strategies to improve supply chain responsiveness and continuity.

The analysis focused on inventory availability, demand consistency, production delays, and supply chain model suitability.

---

## My Role
- Analyzed inventory, customer order, production, and PSI datasets
- Identified the SKU experiencing stockout issues
- Evaluated operational supply chain requirements
- Assessed suitable supply chain models for reducing future stockouts
- Recommended analytics-driven operational improvements

---

## Key Findings

| Analysis Area | Result |
|---|---|
| SKU Experiencing Stockouts | FP2020 |
| Supply Chain Requirement | Flexibility |
| Supply Chain Requirement | Speed |
| Supply Chain Requirement | Cost-efficiency |
| Recommended Supply Chain Model | Agile |
| Recommended Supply Chain Model | Flexible |

---

## Key Insight
The analysis showed that lean supply chain operations may struggle during production disruptions without sufficient flexibility and responsiveness. Agile and flexible supply chain models can improve resilience and reduce stockout risk for delayed SKUs.

---

## Recommendations
- Conduct demand forecasting analysis to improve replenishment planning
- Use predictive maintenance techniques to reduce production disruptions
- Monitor operational data using IoT-enabled real-time tracking
- Analyze customer feedback to improve product and service performance
- Increase operational flexibility for high-demand SKUs
